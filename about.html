ABOUT

NEUD New York exists for the moments that matter. Not routines. Not long timelines. We create event-ready essentials that work fast, because the moments that matter deserve your full presence. After travel. Before the meeting. Nights that run longer than planned. The right product, in the right moment, shifts everything—how you feel, how you look, how you show up. NEUD is a neuroaesthetic-inspired brand built on one simple belief: the right product, in the right moment, changes everything.

Dr. Yelena Yeretsky. A New York-based physician with 25 years of experience in medical aesthetics, Dr. Y helped define the field long before it entered the mainstream. Her West Village practice has become a trusted destination for advanced skin treatments and a clientele that expects results. The people in her chair kept asking the same question: what do I do tonight to look good by tomorrow? That answer became NEUD — event-ready essentials designed to make you look and feel good for the moments that matter.

NEUD formulas are developed with one philosophy: to look good and feel good, quickly. Guided by neuroaesthetics, each detail is considered with intention: the way a texture feels, the subtle trace of scent, the moment something registers not just on the surface, but across the senses. It’s immediate, visible, and makes you feel ready for your moment.
